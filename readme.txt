Thank you for installing Kinger Ukagaka/Ghost

Kinger has a playful, eccentric, and somewhat clumsy personality.

He also comes with a couple of games that you can play with him.

Besides being fun company, he also has features to help you with your work or studies.

Features

1. Rock, paper, scissors game.
2. Watch videos together.
3. Usage summary.
4. To-do list.
5. Empty the recycle bin.

This is a WIP (work in progress), as I still plan to add more features. Not many more, but it will take me some time.
I'm only human :v

Planned future features:
-Point system
-Kinger abstraction
-Alarm
-A list to keep track of episodes from the user's shows

I'd love to hear any comments, questions, or suggestions you may have. You can contact me on X (Twitter):
@TakagiChikita or @soykyon

Have fun.
